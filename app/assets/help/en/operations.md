# Operations

The list of all the operations of the accounts you can see, newest first.

## Find an operation

- **Search** in the label.
- **Filters**: "To classify", "Not cleared", "Subscriptions", by category (sub-categories included), by account.

## An operation's sheet

Tapping an operation opens its sheet:

- **Category**: pick it from the list, or type a few letters. "Create the category" adds one without leaving the sheet.
- **Always classify like this**: creates a rule on this label and classifies the similar operations at once (except those classified by hand).
- **Split between categories**: share one operation between several categories, a supermarket receipt for instance.
- **This is a subscription**: creates the planned operation of every month.
- **This is a loan instalment**: links the debit to an existing loan or declares one.
- **Note**, attached **invoices and receipts**, **past operations** with the same label.
- **Move to trash**: the operation leaves the calculations; "Undo" brings it back.

Where the category comes from is always shown: your choice, a household rule, a common rule, your history, or an AI proposal you accepted.

## Clearing

Clearing an operation means ticking that it does appear on the bank statement.

- Tap the **circle** on the left of an operation to clear or un-clear it.
- **Long press**: selection mode, to clear or un-clear several operations at once.

## Enter an operation

The **"Enter an operation"** button adds an operation by hand, a cheque not yet cashed for instance. It will be recognised when the statement brings it.
