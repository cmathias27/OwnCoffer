# Home

Home shows at a glance where the money stands today and what is coming, on the accounts you can see.

Home always stays on the current month: it answers "is all well today?".

## The widgets

Home is a grid of widgets: two columns on a phone, four on a large screen. A widget is **small** (one cell), **medium** (two cells wide) or **large** (two by two); the larger it is, the more it shows. Tap a widget to open its page.

- **Pépite**: the word of the week, or her whole card when large.
- **All well?** and **To watch**: a one-line summary, then one point per thing to look at, the most urgent first: an account overdrawn or that would be within 30 days, a loan instalment not debited, operations of the month to classify, a subscription whose price went up, new advice. Tapping a point opens the page where it can be dealt with.
- **First steps** (at the beginning): create an account, import a statement, classify the operations. Each step ticks itself from your data; "Hide" removes the widget.
- **Current advice**: the first pieces of advice from **My advisor**.
- **Total of the accounts**, with the **lowest point of the next 30 days**; when large, "Why this figure?" and the balance of the accounts.
- **Accounts**: the balance of each account.
- **Left to live on** until the end of the budget month, and per day; when large, the lines of the calculation.
- **Month summary**: what is left of the month (income minus spending and savings).
- **Forecast**: the lowest point of the 30 days and the curve of the balance of all the accounts in euros.
- **This week**: the due dates of the next 7 days.
- **This month**: spent, received, and when large the categories where you spent the most.
- **Operations**: the latest operations of your accounts.
- **To classify**: the number of operations of the month to classify.
- **Subscriptions**: their total this month and the price rises.
- **Loans**: what is still owed, the monthly payments and the next due date.
- **Invoices**: the invoices not attached to an operation yet.
- **Imports**: the date of the last statement imported, and a button to import one.
- **Notifications**: the unread notifications.
- **Budget goals**: what is left of this month's spending goals, spent against planned, the goals exceeded; when large, the categories closest to their limit.
- **Spending split**: the pie of this month's spending by category, as in Budget; the ring alone when small, with the legend and percentages when medium or large.
- **Last month**: the review of the last month over, what was left against the month before, goals exceeded, bank fees and the biggest expense; a tap opens its Month review.
- **Comparison**: this month against the average of the three months before, the categories going up and down found by the engine.
- **Trends**: income and spending over the last six months as bars (the current month lighter, it is not over), with the average of the months over.
- **Summary**: the next 30 days in brief (lowest point, unread notifications; when large, advice waiting, subscription rises and the curve).

**Every function** can also become a widget, as a small or medium **shortcut**: Search, Enter an operation, Scan a receipt, Categories, Rules, Members, Export my data, Security, Assistant, Apps, Display, Help, Keyboard shortcuts, Give feedback, Server administration. Only the functions you may use are offered (for instance rules outside simple mode, administration for the server administrator).

The small **?** of a widget says what it shows and leads to the matching help; **"Why this figure?"**, under a key figure, shows the lines of the calculation. Every figure is the engine's, as on its page.

## Edit home

Tap **Edit home**, at the top of the page, or long-press a widget. The grid shows:

- **drag** a widget by its handle (✥) to any cell; empty cells stay empty;
- the button at the bottom right **changes its size**; the menu (…) **moves** it by one cell (up, down, left, right) or picks its size, handy with a keyboard;
- the **cross** removes it;
- **Add a widget** opens the list of the widgets not shown, the page summaries then the shortcuts to functions: tap one, or pick its size straight away;
- **Reset** offers the original home page, **Cancel** forgets the changes, **Done** keeps them.

A widget put or enlarged over others pushes them down. The layout is kept **on this device**, one for the phone and one for the large screen; nothing is sent to the server.

## Good to know

- Planned amounts only count what is declared (planned operations, subscriptions, loans). A forecast balance remains an estimate.
- The link to **My advisor** leads to OwnCoffer's proposals (goals to set or adjust).

## Pépite, the accounts hamster

**Pépite** helps you do your accounts **once a week**, in about ten minutes, in four steps: import the latest statements, check the operations, look at the forecast, then **celebrate**. Pépite **ticks on her own** what she sees — a statement imported, a transaction checked or changed, the forecast opened — and **counts the week as soon as all three are done**, without waiting for "Celebrate". She also announces the badges earned along the way. "Open" leads to the page of the step; tick the box if the step has nothing to do this week (no new statement, for instance).

- Each week done gives **10 seeds**, each badge **5 seeds**. Weeks count, not days in a row: a missed week erases nothing and the total earned never goes down.
- **Pépite's shop** (in **Pépite and my reserve**): seeds are spent on **accessories** (scarf, glasses, beanie…, one worn at a time) and items for its **burrow** (cushion, wheel, hut…, as many as you like). Each item is bought once; the seeds to spend show next to the total earned, which does not go down. The **backpack** is bought in the Android or Windows app, then shows everywhere. Seeds are worth nothing else and can be exchanged for nothing.
- **Your year with Pépite** (in **Pépite and my reserve**, from December 1st; on the home page in January): weeks of accounts, seeds, badges and challenges of the year, goals kept (in % of the months followed) and the best kept category, subscriptions stopped and what they saved. Worked out from your own doings and the accounts you see; never compared to anyone.
- **The challenge of the month** (in **Pépite and my reserve**), optional: pick one from the list (classify 20 operations, attach 5 invoices, create 3 rules, declare 2 subscriptions, set yourself a goal). Pépite counts by herself what you do from the moment you chose it, on all your devices; done, it brings **10 seeds**, once a month. Dropped or unfinished: nothing is lost. "Offer a challenge every month" hides it on this device.
- **Badges to earn**: shown as silhouettes in **Pépite and my reserve**, with a hint to discover OwnCoffer's features (sorting rules, scheduled operations, invoices, loans, subscriptions, security, reconciliation, goals in %, invited members). No badge depends on an amount.
- **Later** hides the reminder until the next day.
- In **Settings**, **Pépite and my reserve** shows the seeds and badges, and sets Pépite up on this device: shown or not, and a **reminder by notification** (Android and Windows, off by default) on the day and at the time you choose.
- In a browser, as long as you have never had a session in the Android or Windows app, Pépite suggests them: a card on the home page (one day a week at most), a word at the end of the ritual, a banner on an Android phone (recognised by the browser itself, nothing is sent). **Later** forgets it for a week, **Don't suggest again** for good; nothing while an account is overdrawn. The first session in an app earns the **Pépite on the go** badge.
- **Getting started**: the list of what makes the app useful (first statement, rule, scheduled operation, goal, security, backup, app, reminder, members), ticked from your data; "Open" leads to each step. A card on the home page suggests it while steps are left; "Not now" removes it, the list stays in **Pépite and my reserve**. The server administrator also finds their own there: backups, restore test, automatic updates, invitations, AI, proxy.
- **Did you know?**: on the first visit of a page, a tip about that page, one a day at most. "Got it" or "Learn more" (the help page), and it never comes back, even on another device. **Show the tips**, in Pépite and my reserve, turns them off on this device.
- What Pépite says is chosen from your accounts, never by an AI. In the standalone version, the reserve stays on the device and follows the move to a server.
