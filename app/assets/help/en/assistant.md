# Assistant (AI, optional)

OwnCoffer works fully without AI. The assistant is **off by default**: everyone chooses to turn it on.

## Turn it on

1. The server administrator adds a provider in **Server administration > AI** (Ollama on your network, Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude…).
2. Here, choose the **provider** and the **model**, then turn the assistant on.

## What it can do

- **Answer your questions** about your accounts: "how much did I spend in restaurants this month?", "which due dates are coming?".
- **Classify the operations to classify**: the shortcut asks for you. It proposes a category and, for a merchant that comes back, a rule.
- **Read a document** (with a model that reads images): attach the photo of an amortisation table for it to propose the loan. Receipts, invoices and loan documents already kept in OwnCoffer can also be read directly, from their menu ("Read with the AI" button).

## Its limits, on purpose

- It reads your figures **only through OwnCoffer's functions**, on the accounts you can see. It computes no amount itself.
- It **changes nothing**: its proposals go to **My advisor**, and you accept them or not.
- Each answer says which functions were consulted. With an online provider, what was sent to it is shown.
- If the provider does not answer, it is said plainly: OwnCoffer never tries another one instead.

## From another assistant (MCP)

You can also query OwnCoffer from an assistant you already use (Claude Desktop or any MCP client): create a **personal token** in this page. It acts with your rights, can be revoked at any time, and only makes proposals.
