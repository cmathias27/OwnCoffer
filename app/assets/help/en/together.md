# Together

Expenses shared between the household's members, and the common pot.

## Shared expenses

Write down who paid what, and for whom (every member by default, in equal parts). OwnCoffer keeps the tally and suggests **as few repayments as possible** to settle up: "Alex owes Sam €42".

- **"Repayment"** records money given back from one member to another: it brings the tally up to date.
- A wrong line can be removed; everything stays in the history.

## Common pot

Enter the amount to pay into the joint account each month, then each member's income (tap a member to enter it). Each share is **proportional to income**; while no income is known, the pot is split equally.

The calculations are made by OwnCoffer's engine, to the cent, without AI.
