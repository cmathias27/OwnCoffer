# Getting started

OwnCoffer keeps your bank accounts at home: it reads the statements you download from your bank's website, classifies the operations and forecasts your balance. It never connects to your bank.

## In five steps

1. **Create your accounts** (Money › Accounts): name, opening balance and, if you have one, your authorised overdraft as the alert threshold.
2. **Import a statement** (Papers › Import, or the **+** button): download it as OFX, QIF or CSV from the bank's website, then drop it here. A preview shows what will be added before anything is saved.
3. **Classify what is left** (Money › Operations, "To classify" filter): most operations already are. For a merchant that comes back, "Always classify like this" creates a rule.
4. **Declare what comes back every month**: salary, rent, subscriptions, loans. They enter the forecast balance.
5. **Set a few goals** in the Budget, for groceries or going out for instance.

## Finding your way

The app fits in **five sections**, at the bottom on a phone and on the left on a large screen:

- **Home**: is all well today?
- **Money**: accounts and operations;
- **Plan**: expected balance, budget, loans;
- **Papers**: invoices and import;
- **Advisor**: My advisor, with the number of new pieces of advice since your last visit.

A section with several screens shows them as tabs. At the top, the **trail** (for instance "Plan › Loans") says where you are, and going back leads to the parent screen.

- **Search** (the magnifier at the top) finds at once operations of all your accounts, loans, invoices and the app's features ("export", "password"…). Offline, only the features are offered.
- The **+** button adds an operation, scans a receipt or imports a statement.
- The **bell** gathers the notifications, with filters: alerts, advice, system.
- **Settings** (the cog) gathers the rest: categories and rules, export, members, security, apps, Pépite, administration, help and feedback, and the **display** of this device: **simple mode** (hides the advanced features, without turning anything off), **large text**, **high contrast**.
- On a phone, **swiping an operation** to the right classifies it, to the left opens it.
- On Windows and the web, with the keyboard: `/` to search, `N` for a new operation, `1` to `5` for the sections, `?` for the list of shortcuts.

## Then

- Import a new statement whenever you like, every week for instance. Operations already there are recognised and left out.
- **Home** sums up where the money stands today and what is coming; its **First steps** card follows the first three steps.
- The **?** button at the top of each page opens the help for that page.

## A problem, an idea?

**"Give feedback"** (in Settings) sends a message to your server's administrator.

## Undo

After most actions, an **"Undo"** button shows at the bottom for a few seconds: it puts things back as they were.

## On Windows or Android

Settings → **"Apps"**: the Windows and Android apps to download, in your server's version. When the server is updated, a banner says so in the app, and **"Install"** downloads the new version from GitHub, checks it against the published checksums and installs it (Android asks to confirm; Windows closes and starts again). In standalone mode, "Look for a new version" on that page. Nothing is downloaded unless you ask.

On Windows and Android, OwnCoffer can also be used **alone on the device**, without a server: that is the first launch choice. "Change mode" (Settings) goes back to it.
