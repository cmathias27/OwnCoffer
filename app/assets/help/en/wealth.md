# Net worth

What you own, your regulated savings, the health of your finances and the year's review.

## Net worth

The balance of the accounts you can see, plus the **assets** you declare (home, vehicle, investments, valuables), minus what is left to repay on your loans. An asset has a value and the date of that estimate: update it now and then.

## Regulated savings

An administrator says which savings accounts are a **Livret A**, an **LDDS**, a **LEP** or a **Livret jeune**, and their rate. OwnCoffer then shows:

- the balance against the **ceiling** and the room left;
- the **interest** estimated under the fortnight rule: a deposit counts from the next fortnight, a withdrawal is taken off from the current one; the interest earned so far this year, and over the whole year if nothing moves any more.

These are estimates: only your bank is authoritative.

## Financial health

A score out of 100, the average of four indicators computed over your last complete months:

- **Emergency savings**: how many months of spending your savings accounts cover (good from 3 months);
- **Savings rate**: the share of your income left over (good from 10 %);
- **Debt**: your loan instalments against your income (good up to 30 %, usual bank limit 35 %);
- **Regularity**: the months ended without spending more than earned.

An indicator without enough data is marked "unknown" and does not count in the score.

## Yearly review

For the chosen year: income, spending, savings, estimated interest on regulated savings, and the biggest spending categories.

The **Tax return** part gathers the operations of the categories you mark as relevant for taxes (donations, childcare, home help…) with **"Categories for taxes"**. OwnCoffer does not compute the tax: it gives you the amounts to check.
