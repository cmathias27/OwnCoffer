# Accounts

An OwnCoffer account matches a bank account: current account, savings account, joint account…

## Create an account

- **Name**: as you like, "Joint account" for instance.
- **Opening balance**: the balance of the account on the date of the first operation you will import. The calculation starts from it.
- **Alert threshold**: often the authorised overdraft. OwnCoffer warns you when the forecast balance will go below it.
- **Visibility**: an account can be **restricted** to some members of the household. The others see neither the account, nor its operations, nor its amounts in the budget.

## What the list shows

For each account: the **current balance**, the **cleared balance** (only the operations you have cleared) and the **lowest point of the next 30 days**. Tapping an account opens its operations.

## Check the balance

**"Check the balance"**: enter the balance shown by the bank on a date. OwnCoffer compares it with the calculated balance and, if they differ, shows what may explain it: operations entered by hand and not cleared, or a missing statement.

If the balance has been wrong from the start, correct the account's **opening balance**.
