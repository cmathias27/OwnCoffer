# Projects

Put money aside for a project, and spread the big yearly expenses so they no longer land all at once.

## Savings projects

A project has a name, an amount to reach, and optionally a date and the account where the money is kept.

- The bar shows how far you are; **"Put aside"** adds what you have just saved (a negative amount takes it back).
- With a date, OwnCoffer works out **how much to put aside each month** to get there in time. A date passed without the amount reached is flagged.
- A finished or dropped project can be **archived**: it stays visible at the bottom of the list.

## Spread expenses

Planned operations that come less often than monthly (yearly insurance, property tax, quarterly servicing…) are picked up automatically. For each one:

- the **monthly amount** to put aside to have it on the day;
- what should **already be put aside** today, given the time since the previous one.

The total at the top adds up what to put aside each month for your projects and spread expenses.

## Where the figures come from

Every amount is computed by OwnCoffer's financial engine, never by an AI. Only the accounts you can see count.
