# Invoices

Keep invoices and receipts with the operation that paid them, and find them the day a warranty is needed.

## Attach an invoice

- In an operation's sheet: **"Attach an invoice"** (PDF or image, 10 MB at most) or **"Take a photo"**.
- A photo is **cropped as by a scanner**: the sheet is found, you adjust the corners, it is straightened and made easier to read. Everything happens on your device.
- The file name is made for you: date, merchant, description, amount.

## Invoice without an operation

An invoice often comes before the statement. **"Add an invoice"** in this page keeps it **to attach**. OwnCoffer then proposes the matching operations (amount, date, merchant), and the sheet of a new operation offers "Attach here". Nothing is attached without your choice.

## Read with the AI

With the assistant on and a model that reads images, an invoice's menu offers to **read it with the AI**:

- **receipt joined to an operation**: its lines are grouped by category, and the operation's **split** is proposed. What the lines do not explain (discount, rounding, unreadable line) becomes a part to classify, computed by OwnCoffer;
- **invoice waiting**: its amount, date and merchant are proposed, to attach it faster.

The AI only reports what is written; OwnCoffer checks it and does the calculations. Everything goes to **My advisor** and only applies with your agreement. With an online provider, OwnCoffer first asks whether you agree to send the document.

## Warranty

Enter the end of the warranty (1, 2, 3 or 5 years, or a date). A **reminder** comes a month before, in the notifications and by email. The "Warranty running" filter finds them.

## Share and export

- **Send by email** an invoice as an attachment (if the server can send emails).
- **Export as ZIP** the invoices shown, under their names.

An invoice is only visible to those who can see the account of its operation.
