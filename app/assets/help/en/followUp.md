# Follow-up

What not to forget: reminders, expected refunds, contracts to cancel in time, and events.

## Reminders

A note and a date ("Send the claim form", "Compare car insurance"). On the day, a notification reminds you. Your reminders are **personal**: other members do not see them.

## Expected refunds

Money owed to you: health insurance, a returned purchase, a loan to a relative. Enter the amount and since when you expect it, and optionally a due date and the account it will reach.

As soon as a **credit of the same amount** arrives after that date on your accounts, the refund is marked received by itself. Received otherwise (cash, cheque not yet imported): **"Mark received"**. After the due date, a notification lets you know.

## Contracts

Attach a renewal date and a notice period to a planned operation (internet box, insurance, subscription). OwnCoffer works out the **last day to cancel** and lets you know during the month before.

## Events

A wedding, holidays, a move: create the event with its dates and, if you like, a budget, then attach the operations of the period. You see what it cost, even spread over several accounts and categories.
