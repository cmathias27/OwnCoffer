# Loans

Your loans and instalment payments, what is left to repay and your debt ratio.

## Add a loan

- From a debit, the simplest way: in the operation's sheet, **"This is a loan instalment"**. The form is pre-filled, past instalments are found and a rule will classify the next ones.
- Or by hand: lender, type, principal, rate, duration, first due date, insurance, debit account.
- **"Found in your operations"** lists the instalment payments (Oney, Alma, Klarna…) and small loans debited recently that are not declared yet.

The **debited instalment** is what counts: the table, the remaining principal and the end date start from it.

## What a loan shows

- **Amortisation table**, remaining principal, interest paid and to pay, total cost, end date.
- The **debits** already linked and the total paid.
- **Simulated early repayment**: interest saved and new end date.
- **Documents**: amortisation table, loan offer, screenshots, kept on your server.

With a debit account, the instalments enter the **forecast balance**.

## Debt ratio

Instalments divided by the average income of the last three months, with a mark at 35 %. It is indicative: your bank's calculation is what counts.
