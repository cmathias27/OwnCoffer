# Loans

Your loans and instalment payments, what is left to repay and your debt ratio.

## Add a loan

- From a debit, the simplest way: in the operation's sheet, **"This is a loan instalment"**. The form is pre-filled, past instalments are found and a rule will classify the next ones.
- Or by hand: lender, type, principal, rate, duration, first due date, insurance, debit account.
- **"Found in your operations"** lists the instalment payments (Oney, Alma, Klarna…) and small loans debited recently that are not declared yet.

The **debited instalment** is what counts: the table, the remaining principal and the end date start from it.

## What a loan shows

- **Amortisation table**, remaining principal, interest paid and to pay, total cost, end date.
- The **debits** already linked and the total paid.
- **Simulated early repayment**: interest saved and new end date.
- **Documents**: amortisation table, loan offer, screenshots, kept on your server.

With a debit account, the instalments enter the **forecast balance**.

## Changes: variable rate, renegotiation

In a loan's sheet, **"Add a change"**:

- **New rate** (variable rate): from a date. The end date is kept and the instalment computed again; if the debited instalment is known, it is kept and the end date moves.
- **Renegotiation**: new rate, and a new instalment or a new remaining duration.
- **Early repayment**: an amount repaid on top; the instalment is kept, the loan ends sooner.

The table, the remaining principal, the planned instalments and the balance forecast follow. A change is removed in one tap. A change that would never let the loan be repaid is refused.

## Revolving credit

Choose the **"Revolving credit"** type: enter the **amount used** today, the debited **instalment**, the rate and, if you like, the **credit limit**. The duration follows from them. Each **new use** is added in "Changes"; the sheet shows what is still **available**. A use over the limit is refused.

## Read a document with the AI

With the assistant on and a model that reads images, the ✨ button of a loan document has it read: the terms it states (rate, duration, instalment, insurance…) are **proposed** in My advisor, with before and after. Nothing changes without your agreement.

## Debt ratio

Instalments divided by the average income of the last three months, with a mark at 35 %. It is indicative: your bank's calculation is what counts.
