# Budget

The Budget shows where the money goes over a period: week, month, quarter or year.

## The page, from top to bottom

- **Summary**: income, spending, savings and what is left, compared with the previous period.
- **Left to live on** until the next pay, and per day.
- **Operations to classify**, with a button to classify them.
- **Goals of the month**: one gauge per category with a goal. The mark shows where you should be today; "At this pace" announces the end of the month.
- **Spending and income by category**, as lines, table, pie chart or trend chart. Tapping a category shows its sub-categories, then its operations.

## The budget month

A household administrator can make the **month start on pay day** (from the 1st to the 28th), from the 27th to the 26th for instance.

## Goals

A goal is a monthly amount for a spending category. OwnCoffer warns you at **80 %**, at **100 %**, and **as soon as the projection announces an overrun**. A suggested goal is offered from the three previous months.

## Shortcuts

- **Month review**: rises and falls, goals exceeded, bank fees, biggest expenses, subscriptions that changed.
- **Due-date calendar** of the month, with their state.
- **Subscriptions**: their cost per month and per year.
- **Compare**: categories, periods or accounts with each other, and the **simulation of a contract change** (savings over 1 to 10 years, month when the change pays off).
- **Analyses**: how reliable the forecasts were (planned operations that came as planned, estimated variable spending against reality), unusual operations (much more than usual, or charged twice) and the bank fees of the last 12 months.
- **My advisor**: the goals you could set or adjust, and the points to watch.
