# Household members

A household (or group) gathers the people who manage the same accounts: a couple, a family.

## Roles

- **Reader**: looks, without changing anything.
- **User**: enters, classifies and imports.
- **Administrator**: also manages the accounts, the members and the start of the budget month.

## Invite someone

Sign-up is **by invitation only**. A household administrator enters the person's address and role; they receive a link valid for 7 days. An invitation can be revoked.

## Household log

The household's administrators have a **log** button in this page: who did what in the household, and when (accounts, imports, operations, rules, loans, members…). The content of the changes is not shown.

## Restricted accounts

An account can be kept for some members (a personal account, for instance). The others do not see it, nor its operations, nor its amounts in the totals, budgets, alerts or the assistant's answers.
