# Notifications

The bell at the top of the screen gathers what OwnCoffer wants to tell you; the number shows the unread notifications.

## What is reported

- **Forecast overdraft**: an account will go below its alert threshold (also by email).
- **Goals**: a category reaches 80 %, then 100 % of its goal, or the projection announces an overrun.
- **End of warranty** of an invoice, a month before (also by email).

Each alert is sent only once for the same threshold and period, and only covers the accounts you can see.

## Emails

Emails are only sent if the server administrator has set up sending (SMTP). Otherwise, notifications stay in the app.
