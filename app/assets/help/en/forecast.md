# Forecast

The balance curve day by day, over 30 days, 90 days or a year, for each account.

## What is counted

- The **planned operations**: salary, rent, subscriptions, loan instalments. You add them here or from an operation's sheet ("This is a subscription").
- Optionally, the **estimated variable spending** (groceries, fuel, going out…): for each category, the median of the last three complete months. Tap "Including … of estimated variable spending" to see the calculation. The "Variable spending" filter removes it.

When a real operation arrives, it is **matched** with the planned one; a due date that does not come is shown as **late**.

## Overdraft alert

If the forecast balance goes below the account's **alert threshold**, OwnCoffer warns you in the app and by email. The alert only counts planned operations, not estimated spending.

## Simulate a purchase

**"Simulate a purchase"**: amount, purchase date, and the date on which you want to know your balance. Five cards compare **without the purchase**, **cash**, **in 3 instalments**, **in 4 instalments** and **next month**: balance on the chosen date, lowest point, overdraft or goal exceeded. Nothing is saved until you choose.

A forecast remains an estimate: the more complete your planned operations, the closer it is.
