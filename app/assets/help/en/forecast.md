# Expected balance

The balance curve day by day, over 30 days, 90 days or a year, for each account. The account chosen is kept on this device, for the operations too.

**"Why this figure?"**, under the end balance, shows the calculation: today's balance, plus the planned income, minus the planned debits, plus the rest (estimated variable spending).

## What is counted

- The **planned operations**: salary, rent, subscriptions, loan instalments. You add them here or from an operation's sheet ("This is a subscription").
- Optionally, the **estimated variable spending** (groceries, fuel, going out…): for each category, the median of the last three complete months. Tap "Including … of estimated variable spending" to see the calculation. The "Variable spending" filter removes it.

## Pay found

If your pay comes into the account every month (same label, similar amount, around the same day, at least three months in a row) but is not a planned operation yet, the **"Pay found"** card proposes it. **"Add to the forecast"** opens the form already filled in (usual amount, day, recognised label, amount tolerance): check it, then save. **"Dismiss"** stops proposing it on this device. An operation classified as "Salary" comes first; otherwise, the largest regular income.

## Month end

The curve marks each **month end** with a dashed line, and the forecast balance at the next month end is written under the end balance. If your household's month does not start on the 1st (group setting, often payday), choose the marker: **"Budget month"** (the day before the household's month starts) or **"Calendar month"** (the last day of the month). The choice is kept on this device.

When a real operation arrives, it is **matched** with the planned one; a due date that does not come is shown as **late**.

## Overdraft alert

If the forecast balance goes below the account's **alert threshold**, OwnCoffer warns you in the app and by email. The alert only counts planned operations, not estimated spending.

## Simulate a purchase

**"Simulate a purchase"**: amount, purchase date, and the date on which you want to know your balance. Five cards compare **without the purchase**, **cash**, **in 3 instalments**, **in 4 instalments** and **next month**: balance on the chosen date, lowest point, overdraft or goal exceeded. Nothing is saved until you choose.

A forecast remains an estimate: the more complete your planned operations, the closer it is.
