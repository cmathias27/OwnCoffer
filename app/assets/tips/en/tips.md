# Did you know?

Pépite's tips (decision 0028): one on the first visit of a screen, at most one a day. Each tip starts with a line `## code · page`, the page being the help's; the code never changes, it keeps the tip from being shown again.

## home_cards · home
Each card of the home page leads to its page: tap "This week" to see what is due in the next 7 days.

## home_ritual · home
Once a week is enough: import the statements, check the operations, look at the forecast, and celebrate. A missed week erases nothing.

## accounts_check_balance · accounts
"Check the balance" compares the balance shown by your bank with the computed one, and shows what may explain a gap.

## accounts_restricted · accounts
An account can be restricted to some members of the household: the others see neither the account nor its operations.

## operations_always · operations
In an operation's details, "Always classify like this" creates a rule and classifies the similar operations at once.

## operations_select · operations
A long press on an operation starts the selection mode, to clear several at once.

## operations_split · operations
"Split between categories" shares an operation, a supermarket receipt for instance, between several categories.

## forecast_simulate · forecast
"Simulate a purchase" compares cash, 3 or 4 instalments and next month, without saving anything.

## forecast_subscription · forecast
In an operation's details, "This is a subscription" creates the planned operation of each month: the forecast gets more accurate.

## import_ofx · import
The OFX format is the one to choose: it recognises the account by itself and gives the bank's balance, to compare.

## import_many · import
"Several files" imports several statements at once, even of several accounts.

## import_overlap · import
A statement that overlaps the previous one counts nothing twice: duplicates are recognised and left out.

## categories_tester · categories
The rule tester shows how a label would be cleaned and classified, without saving anything.

## categories_manual · categories
A choice made by hand is never overwritten by a rule.

## budget_month_start · budget
An administrator of the household can make the budget month start on pay day.

## budget_review · budget
The "Month review" sums up the rises, the falls, the goals overrun and the subscriptions that changed.

## loans_from_operation · loans
In a direct debit's details, "This is a loan instalment" fills the loan in and finds the past instalments.

## loans_spotted · loans
"Found in your operations" lists the payments in instalments not declared yet.

## invoices_scan · invoices
On a phone, "Scan with the camera" crops the invoice as a scanner would, without it leaving your device.

## invoices_warranty · invoices
Give an invoice's end of warranty: a reminder comes a month before.
